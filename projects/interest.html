Tekken

Tekken is a fighting game I enjoyed playing with others.

Tekken is a popular Japanese media franchise centered on a series of 3D fighting video games developed and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. First launched in arcades in 1994 and brought to the PlayStation in 1995, the series is famous for pioneering smooth 3D character animations and mapping individual controller buttons to a fighter's specific limbs. The overarching plot primarily follows the dramatic, multigenerational blood feud and vendettas within the Mishima family as various characters compete in the King of Iron Fist Tournament.
