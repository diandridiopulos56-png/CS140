Favorite character in tekken

Steve Fox

One of the playable character in tekken is Steve Fox, his fighting style is boxing, world boxing champion. Steve Fox is a fictional British middleweight boxing world champion from Bandai Namco's Tekken fighting game series, first introduced in Tekken 4. Adopted at an early age, his overarching storyline revolves around uncovering the mysteries of his past, including a prominent scar on his arm resulting from human experimentation.
